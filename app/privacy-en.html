Privacy policy

Vitamin Compass · last updated: 18 November 2026 · Ελληνικά

Summary

The app does not ask for an account, does not collect personal data, does not show ads and does not track how you use it.

What is stored on your device

  • The entries you open and the results of your searches, so they open quickly and work offline. They stay only on your device and can be deleted with the "Delete saved data" button.
  • Your settings: the maximum storage space and the app language.

What is sent over the internet

  • When you search for a term that is not in the database, the term is sent to public services to find studies and comments: Wikipedia, PubMed (NCBI), Europe PMC, Hacker News, Stack Exchange and Lemmy. Only the term is sent, with nothing that identifies you.
  • If enough studies are found, the term is sent to our own service (Cloudflare) so it can be added to the shared database for everyone. The service uses your IP address temporarily, only to limit abuse (up to 10 requests an hour), and does not store it permanently.
  • The app's data is loaded from GitHub Pages. These services may log technical information (e.g. IP address) according to their own policies.

Health

The app provides general information from scientific studies and user experiences. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose and it does not replace your doctor or pharmacist. You do not enter, and the app does not store, any of your own medical information.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not collect data from them.
