## Full description (up to 4000 characters)
Vitamin Compass shows you what scientific studies and other people's experiences say about vitamins, supplements, foods and habits, ranked from the best supported to the least.

FOUR WAYS TO SEARCH
• Vitamin or supplement: where it may help, which forms exist, which foods contain it.
• Health problem: which vitamins, foods and habits may help, and what to avoid.
• Lifestyle and diet: e.g. "more stamina" → exercise, quitting smoking, sleep, beetroot juice.
• Ask: answers to 240 common questions, e.g. "When is the best time to take vitamin D?".

THREE RESULT VIEWS
• Benefits: first from studies with an evidence grade (A strong, B moderate, C weak), then how often each effect is repeated in user comments.
• Studies: meta-analyses, randomised trials and smaller studies from PubMed, with links.
• Foods and supplements: what to eat instead of a supplement, which supplement forms exist and what precautions are needed.

SAFETY FIRST
• Warnings about interactions with medicines (anticoagulants, thyroxine, antidepressants and more).
• An "Avoid" section for conditions such as thalassaemia, G6PD deficiency and kidney disease.
• For serious conditions no doses are suggested, only topics to discuss with your doctor.

ALWAYS UP TO DATE
• Over 900 curated entries: 220 supplements, 240 health conditions, 200 habits and foods, 240 questions.
• Updated daily with new studies and comments.
• Search for anything: if something is missing, the app searches the sources live and adds it to the shared database for everyone.

NO ACCOUNT, NO ADS
No personal data is collected. Works offline for everything you have already viewed.

IMPORTANT
The app provides general information and does not replace your doctor or pharmacist. Before starting any supplement, especially if you take medicines, are pregnant or have a chronic condition, talk to a health professional.
